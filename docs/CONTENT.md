# Content Model

## Current principle

Content should remain easy to replace without restructuring presentation code.

Do not invent:

- staff identities
- client names
- testimonials
- case-study outcomes
- accreditations
- partnerships
- statistics
- legal claims
- service claims not supplied by the client

Clearly mark placeholders.

## Placeholder policy

Clearly identify missing content and assets as placeholders. Do not invent realistic customer quotes, endorsements, staff biographies, results, client logos or qualifications. Final copy, contact details, images and media have not been supplied in this bootstrap session.
